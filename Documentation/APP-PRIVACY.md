# LINART app privacy

Updated September 25, 2026. This notice describes the LINART iPhone/iPad app operated by Linart Construction Inc. Contact: services@linartinc.com.

## Planning on your device

Browsing the bundled portfolio requires no account. Saved project identifiers and checklist progress are stored locally. Your Project Studio stores notes, links, selected project ideas and copies of photos in protected app files. Photos are resized and their embedded metadata is removed during import. The system photo picker grants access only to the images you choose; the app does not request access to your entire photo library, contacts or location.

Inquiry drafts remain in memory unless you enable saving on the device. Saved files and preferences may be included in device backups. Clear the Studio or use More → Settings → Clear all local app data to remove local planning information. Sign-in credentials are held separately in the device Keychain, which can survive reinstalling the app; sign out or clear local app data to remove this app's saved session.

## Information you choose to send

A project inquiry sends your name, email, telephone, project city or ZIP, service, preferred contact method, timing and description to LINART's existing website inquiry service. That service also receives connection information such as IP address and submission time. Inquiry processing and follow-up are separate from the Studio service.

Direct Studio submission is optional. After verifying your email and consenting, your project answers, references, selected portfolio ideas and chosen photo copies are stored for LINART in Supabase. The app uses dedicated private storage and app-specific tables within LINART's existing Supabase project. Authentication records include your email and user identifier. LINART uses its Hostinger mailbox to deliver sign-in emails. Supabase and Hostinger process service and security information needed to operate these features.

Your Studio receipt confirms secure storage, not an appointment, estimate, team review or response deadline. The app does not automatically link a Studio to a website inquiry.

## Sharing with other services

PDF export prepares a file containing the answers and photos included in the selected format. You choose whether and where to share it. Other apps and recipients apply their own privacy practices. The app cannot confirm that a shared email or PDF arrived. Prepared exports are removed when sharing finishes or during later cleanup; copies saved or received elsewhere are outside that cleanup.

## Retention and deletion

Local data stays until cleared or removed with the app, subject to device backup behavior. Direct app uploads remain until deleted; there is no automatic retention expiry. Sign in and open Send to LINART → Your app submissions to delete completed or unfinished uploads and their photos.

Delete my app account removes app uploads and records an account-deletion request. New uploads are disabled while the request is pending. LINART completes the request within seven days and confirms by email, reviewing shared LINART records separately. This does not clear the local Studio automatically. Contact services@linartinc.com about an earlier website inquiry or information already downloaded by the team. Deletion of active records does not recall third-party copies or immediately erase provider backups/security logs.

## Tracking and diagnostics

The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites. A diagnostics summary is prepared only when you choose to share it; it contains app version and recent error categories, without names, contact details, photographs, notes or sign-in tokens.
